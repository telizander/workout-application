07

Stopwatch

The signature interaction. Digits do not fade or slide — they flip, like the split-flap boards this whole language borrows from. Each leaf catches and loses light as it turns, the case clicks on impact, and the card takes a small mechanical bounce when it lands. Minutes and seconds flip; hundredths glow underneath as a plain electronic readout, the way a real chronometer separates its slow mechanical digits from its fast electronic ones.

Live component — this is the app's own markup and stylesheet, not a mock. Behaviour lives in app.js. Under prefers-reduced-motion the digit lands instantly instead of turning.
