# The Training Year

A repeatable year: four 13-week seasons, each with a job. The program shape stays the
same all year; each season turns four dials: **eating, load, volume, exercise menu**.

Seasonal idea from Dan John ("How to Train Through Winter"): fall is for discipline,
winter is heavy, hard and short, spring is the bridge, summer is for play. Adapted
here to a mostly-indoor, home-gym year with a bulk and a cut in it.

---

## Goals

**Long term (every year, for decades)**
- The physique: shoulders, back, arms. Carl Weathers.
- Strength numbers that keep climbing (below).
- **Age well: active with family for life.** Pain-free everywhere (knees, back, hips,
  elbows, shoulders), strong bones, great balance, hard to injure, never frail.
  This outranks every other goal and every number below.

**This winter (by Feb 14, 2027)**
- Strict pull-ups back, pain-free, 10+.
- Sandbag 140lbs feels routine: cleans, shouldering, carries.
- Visibly bigger, without drifting from lean, so the spring cut is short.

**Leanest point of the year:** summer.

## The cycle

| Season | Weeks | Eating | Training | Job |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Fall: discipline** | mid-Aug to mid-Nov | Lean bulk, about +250 kcal | Strict program, few exercises, done exactly as written. Volume for size. | Start the bulk from lean. Weighted pull-ups return. |
| **Winter: heavy** | mid-Nov to mid-Feb | Continue a small surplus (cap it) | Heavy and hard, abbreviated, out the door. Fewer lifts, heavier, lower reps; EMOM-style work. | Strength peak. Sandbag moves up a bag. |
| **Spring: cut** | mid-Feb to mid-May | Deficit, 0.5-1% bodyweight a week. Lent (starts mid-Feb) = give one thing up. | Keep the heavy lifts heavy with fewer sets. Conditioning comes back: snatch EMOM, carries, density. | Lean by late May. Lose fat, keep every lift. |
| **Summer: hold and play** | mid-May to mid-Aug | Maintenance | Variety and fun: ladders, the 350, new moves, a short block of something new. Test numbers. | Stay lean. Enjoy training. |

The year runs fall → winter → spring → summer, then repeats. A bulk is fall + winter
(about 26 weeks, slow), a cut is spring (13 weeks), summer holds.

## Every season has the same shape

- **4 days a week, about an hour** including warm-up and cool-down.
  Push / Sandbag / Pull / KB Legs + Carries. A rest day between Sandbag and Pull.
- **13 weeks:** three waves of 3 build weeks + 1 lighter week, then a test week (12)
  and a deload (13). Week 12 numbers build the next season.
- **No dead time:** every work block pairs moves that don't compete.
- **Pain rule (any joint):** up to 3/10 that's gone by the next morning is OK. More than
  that, regress the move. Pain lasting 2+ weeks: see a physio.

### Every season keeps these (aging well)

Seasons change the dials; these stay in every week of every season, bulk or cut:

| Keeps | Why | How |
|---|---|---|
| **Heavy loading** | Bone density and muscle are kept by lifting heavy things | Sandbag, double KB, weighted dips. Never a season of only light work. |
| **Achilles and calves** | Achilles history (see below); strong calves and tendons protect the lower leg | Slow heavy calf raises and calf holds, every week |
| **Single-leg and balance** | Balance and single-leg strength are what prevent falls | Lunges, split squats, single-leg deadlifts, Turkish get-ups |
| **Getting up off the floor** | Being able to get down and up easily is a basic marker of aging well | Turkish get-ups, floor work |
| **Carries and grip** | Grip strength tracks long-term health; carries train the whole body | Farmer walks, sandbag carries, hangs |
| **Full range of motion** | Joints stay healthy when they're used through their range | Deep squats, hangs, halos, rope flow |
| **Easy movement on off days** | Walking and moving outside keeps you active between sessions | Walks with family; not counted as training |

### Achilles: the road back to jumping

History: ran exclusively until the Achilles hurt and scar-tissue bumps formed; stopped
before it became a real injury. That's why this kind of training started. Jumping is a
long-term goal, not a current one. No jump squats, hops or running until the stages
below say so. Tendons adapt more slowly than muscle: expect months per stage, not weeks.

| Stage | Work | Move on when |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Build the tendon** (now) | Calf holds (:30-:45, heavy) and slow calf raises (3s up, 3s down), two-leg then one-leg, 2-3 times a week. Ankle mobility. | 25 slow single-leg calf raises each side, pain no more than 2/10, fine the next morning |
| **2. Load it heavier** | Single-leg calf raises holding a KB; slow lowering on the bad side | Same test holding a 16kg KB, with no morning stiffness |
| **3. Low impact** | Pogo hops: small, quiet, two-legged, a few sets of 10. Skipping. | A few weeks with no reaction the next morning |
| **4. Jumps** | Low box jumps landing quietly, broad jumps, then single-leg hops | Each step stays quiet for a few weeks |

Morning stiffness or pain the day after is the signal to step back a stage. A physio
who knows tendons is worth a visit before stage 3.

## Staying close to lean

Every week, same morning: **bodyweight and waist** (tape at the navel).

| Season | Weight | Waist | If it's off |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bulk (fall, winter) | Up 0.25-0.5% a month (about 1lb a month at 200lbs) | Up less than 1 inch over the whole bulk | Waist up 1 inch: back to maintenance for 2-4 weeks |
| This winter | Steady to slightly up | Steady or down | Waist up: drop to maintenance |
| Cut (spring) | Down 0.5-1% a week | Down | Lifts dropping: slow the cut, keep the heavy sets |
| Hold (summer) | Steady | Steady | Adjust a little, don't diet |

If a bulk ends early because the waist hit the limit, start the next cut early.
Never spend more than one season getting back to lean.

## Equipment

- Kettlebells: 12+12, 16+16, 20+20, one 24. Mixed 24+20 pair until a second 24 is affordable.
- Sandbag: up to 160 stuffed; 175 and 200 bags later, when the lifts call for them.
- Rings, bands, 16lb and 20lb dumbbells.
- What it means for programming: kettlebell progress comes from reps, sets, density and
  harder variations more than load. The sandbag is the lift with room to go heavy.

## Starting a new season

1. Run week 12 of the current season and log every number.
2. Copy the last season file in `scripts/seasons/` to a new one (e.g. `spring_2027.py`).
3. Change `SEASON`: dates, eating rule, notes.
4. Turn the dials in the day functions to fit the season (table above). Start each
   lift from the week 12 numbers, never below them.
5. Archive the old `current.json` as `YYYYMMDD.json` (the date the season ended).
6. Point `CURRENT` in `scripts/build_program.py` at the new season and run:
   `python3 scripts/build_program.py`, then `node scripts/validate.js`. Commit.
